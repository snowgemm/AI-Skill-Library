# Project Management

Use when managing schedule, scope, delivery risks, team coordination, and project reporting.

## Workflow

1. Define the outcome, scope boundaries, stakeholders, target date, constraints, and acceptance authority.
2. Break delivery into milestones with tangible outputs, dependency links, owners, and quality gates.
3. Track risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies separately. Give each a likelihood/impact and mitigation.
4. Use estimates as ranges and revise them when new evidence appears.
5. Limit work in progress and surface blocked work early.
6. Report status as completed, in progress, blocked, or unverified based on artifacts and evidence.
7. Manage change requests by documenting effect on scope, schedule, cost, and quality.
8. Close the project with operational handoff, known issues, metrics, and lessons learned.

## Verification checklist

- Scope and success criteria are clear.
- Milestones have verifiable outputs.
- Risks and dependencies have owners.
- Status is evidence-based.
- Handoff and post-launch ownership are defined.

## Reporting

Report source context, decisions, work items, evidence, tests, limitations, and ownership. Do not treat a planning or coordination document as proof that implementation or release work occurred.
