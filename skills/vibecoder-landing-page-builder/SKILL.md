# Landing Page Engineering

Use when creating a product homepage, launch page, portfolio, campaign page, or marketing website.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, value proposition, action goal, evidence available, brand constraints, and required pages.
2. Create a content hierarchy: headline, explanation, benefits, proof, feature details, objection handling, FAQ, and CTA.
3. Verify every factual claim, metric, testimonial, logo, and pricing statement. Do not invent social proof.
4. Inspect existing design tokens and assets. Choose a clear visual system that fits the brand and avoid unnecessary animation or decoration.
5. Implement semantic responsive markup, clear focus states, keyboard support, meaningful alt text, and reduced-motion behavior.
6. Optimize LCP/CLS/INP-relevant assets, fonts, images, scripts, and third-party embeds.
7. Add metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap/robots behavior, structured data only when accurate, and social preview metadata.
8. Test actual CTA destinations, forms, validation, analytics consent, mobile layouts, and production routes.

## Verification checklist

- Messaging is specific and accurate.
- CTA and forms work end-to-end.
- Responsive/accessibility checks pass.
- Performance is measured.
- SEO metadata and links are verified.

## Reporting

Report requirements and assumptions, inspected files, architecture decisions, changes, tests/build results, and unverified areas. Be explicit about where tools, credentials, or human approvals are required. Do not claim an action was completed unless its result is observed.
