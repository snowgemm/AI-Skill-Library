# KPI Readouts and Business Reviews

Use when reporting current performance against KPI targets and explaining what changed, why it matters, and what action follows.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the review period, metric owners, targets, definitions, segments, comparison periods, and data refresh timestamps.
2. Validate the KPI values and denominators. Reconcile differences from prior reports before drafting a narrative.
3. Classify each KPI as on-track, at-risk, below target, or not measurable using the agreed threshold—not an invented status.
4. Explain drivers with segmented or cohort evidence. Distinguish proven drivers from plausible hypotheses.
5. Summarize outcome, variance, leading indicators, guardrails, and operational impact.
6. Convert findings into owners, actions, expected effects, deadlines, and follow-up checks.
7. Keep the scorecard concise while linking to supporting charts, source queries, and methodology.

## Verification checklist

- Targets and thresholds are agreed.
- Figures match source data.
- Driver claims have supporting evidence.
- Hypotheses are labelled.
- Actions have owners and verification criteria.

## Reporting

State the source and version of data/artifacts, filters and definitions, checks performed, assumptions, unresolved limitations, and files delivered. Do not imply the host has connected data, publication, sharing, scheduling, or file-conversion capabilities unless those tools are actually available and report success.
